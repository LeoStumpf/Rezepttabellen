First public release of Rezepttabellen (the app is in German).
• Recipes as recipe tables: ingredients on the left, steps spanning the ingredients they use
• Step cards, cooking mode with timers, scaling, shopping list, pantry and favourites
• Recipe variants, import from websites, photo inbox for recipes from paper
• Works offline with a copy of your collection and syncs with your own server
No ads, no tracking, no account.
