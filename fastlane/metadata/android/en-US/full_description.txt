Rezepttabellen shows your recipes as recipe tables, the format made popular by Cooking for Engineers: ingredients run down the left, steps run to the right, and each step spans exactly the ingredients that go into it. You see at a glance what can start right away and what can wait.

IMPORTANT BEFORE YOU INSTALL
• The app is in German only.
• It needs your own Rezepttabellen server. The server is free, self-hosted software that runs in Docker on a home server, NAS or Raspberry Pi. Without a server the app has nothing to show.
• Setup: enter your server address and, if set, its password, then sync.

READ AND COOK
• Recipe table, step cards or a classic list
• Scale servings, or enter how much of an ingredient you have
• Cooking mode keeps the screen on and lets you tick off steps
• Tap a time in a step to start a timer, with a notification even when the phone is locked
• Print or save a recipe as a one-page A4 PDF
• Share a recipe or its ingredients as text

FIND RECIPES
• Tiles, details or swipeable Discover view
• Search titles, tags and ingredients; filter by diet and time
• Fridge mode: enter what you have and find recipes that use it
• Favourites, sorting and a cooking log with ratings
• Variants of one recipe grouped and compared side by side

PANTRY AND SHOPPING
• Keep track of what you have at home
• See at a glance what a recipe is missing
• Shopping list that sums ingredients across recipes; share it as text

ADD RECIPES
• Editor with live table preview
• Import from recipe websites, also straight from your browser's share menu
• Photograph recipes from books and magazines; the photos go to your server's inbox

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
• Every recipe is stored on your phone and works without a connection
• Changes sync both ways with your server; nothing is lost on conflicts
• Your recipes stay on your server as plain, readable files
• No account, no ads, no analytics. The app talks only to the server you enter.
